<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Engine RPC child process to caller | A child can stall, exit, close only stdout, retain inherited descriptors, emit malformed/faulting output, or ignore cancellation; the driver must distinguish liveness from completion and guarantee bounded teardown without imposing an inference deadline. |
| Conversation interrupt source to subprocess | The existing in-process InterruptFlag crosses from the conversation control path into the engine wait; dropped propagation strands work, while duplicate delivery can race end events or duplicate aborts. |
| Lane policy to local executor and fleet | LaneLimits determines classification, while local JSON persistence and fleet serialization intentionally encode unset duration differently; conflating these can silently cap local work or alter the fleet contract. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-W97-06-01 | Denial of service | Child pipe/process liveness | high | mitigate | Detect EOF, reader faults, and process exit independently of pipe closure; explicitly cancel and force cleanup for an uncooperative child; do not treat elapsed inference time as failure. |
| T-W97-06-02 | Denial of service | Cancellation propagation | high | mitigate | Carry the existing InterruptFlag end-to-end; test the route and queued completion race; abort at most once and reap the child. |
| T-W97-06-03 | Tampering | Lane/fleet serialization boundary | medium | mitigate | Assert local explicit-null persistence and unchanged fleet omission, alongside configured-limit and 600-minute classification cases. |
