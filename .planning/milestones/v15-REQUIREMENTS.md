## Grok preparation

- [x] **GROK-01**: User can exercise both MCP transports, manifest, authentication, approval controls and clean shutdown on an owned local host.

## Out of scope

- Live Grok/account/provider, physical device or external service proof without the required owned prerequisites.
- Production deployment, image publication or tags in this milestone. Repository commits/publication through the established branch and required Ultrathink review/ship gates are separately user-authorized after verification; they do not imply deployment or live Grok proof.
- Signed AgentSwarm dispatch/gates or protected `.swarm/` writes; the user explicitly chose native GSD instead.
- Unrelated dormant seeds, old milestones, architectural re-platforming or upstream modifications.
- Unapproved archival cleanup; the milestone lifecycle retains its separate human gate.
